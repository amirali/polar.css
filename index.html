Typography

This theme keeps contrast soft but readable, using Nord-inspired surfaces and accents. Links, lists, inline code, and block content all work out of the box.

Why classless?

It is ideal for prototypes, docs, personal sites, and MVPs where raw HTML should already feel designed.
